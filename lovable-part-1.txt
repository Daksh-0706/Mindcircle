1.2  CONTRAST CONTRACT - measured, not vibes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Real WCAG 2.1 results. Body text needs 4.5:1. Large text (24px+, or 18.66px
bold+) and UI borders or icons need 3:1.

PASSES - safe for any text:
  ink #2A2A2A on cream 13.63 | on white 14.35
  plum on cream 11.00 | on cream-dark 9.99
  cream / white on plum 11.00 / 11.58
  plum-light on cream 6.84
  cream / white on plum-light 6.84 / 7.20
  ink-muted #6A6865 on cream 5.27
  danger-text on cream 5.35
  terracotta-dark on cream / white 5.75 / 6.06
  sage-dark on cream / white 4.58 / 4.83
  ink-faint #80698A on cream 4.63
  plum on sage-light 5.31
  white on #9A4A34 6.17

FAILS - banned outright:
  sage on cream / white                 2.87 / 3.02
      -> sage-dark for text. sage is fills, rings, dots, chart strokes only.
  cream or white on sage / sage-light   2.87 / 3.02 / 2.18
      -> plum on sage-light (5.31), or a sage/12 tint with sage-dark text.
  warm-gray on cream / white            3.28 / 3.45
      -> ink-muted for secondary text. warm-gray is icons, disabled only.
  danger as body text                   4.16
      -> danger-text for the text, danger for the border and icon.

Also banned: warm-gray-light on cream (2.06) and warm-gray-lighter on cream
(1.24). warm-gray-lighter is a BORDER COLOUR ONLY.

BORDERLINE - large text or UI sizes only:
  terracotta on cream 4.00 | white on terracotta 4.21 |
  sage-dark on a sage/12 tint 3.96
  If a tinted chip needs real text, use plum on the tint (9.26) or darken the
  tint to sage/8.

THE GRADIENT PROBLEM
  plum -> terracotta gives white text 11.58:1 at the plum end but 4.21:1 at the
  terracotta end, so it fails. The seed ends at #9A4A34 instead: 11.58 -> 6.17.
  Use --brand-gradient. Never re-add bare terracotta as a gradient endpoint
  behind text. (plum -> terracotta at 24px+ display type is acceptable.)

LOCKED TEXT COLOUR ASSIGNMENTS
  Body                              -> text-ink
  Secondary, descriptions, helper   -> text-ink-muted
  Meta, timestamps, axis labels     -> text-ink-faint
  Warm emphasis, specialty, streak  -> text-terracotta-dark
  Calm, success, grounded           -> text-sage-dark
  Errors                            -> text-danger-text in border-danger/30
                                       bg-danger/5
  Text on plum or plum-light        -> text-cream
  Text on any sage fill             -> text-plum
  Labels in tinted chips            -> text-plum unless the chip is plum-tinted
