--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2  CONTRAST CONTRACT - measured, not vibes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every ratio below is the real WCAG 2.1 result for these hexes. Body text needs
4.5:1. Large text (24px and above, or 18.66px bold and above) and UI borders or
icons need 3:1.

PASSES - safe for any text:

  ink #2A2A2A       on cream            13.63
  ink               on white            14.35
  plum              on cream            11.00
  plum              on cream-dark        9.99
  cream / white     on plum             11.00 / 11.58
  plum-light        on cream             6.84
  cream / white     on plum-light        6.84 / 7.20
  ink-muted #6A6865 on cream             5.27
  danger-text       on cream             5.35
  terracotta-dark   on cream / white     5.75 / 6.06
  sage-dark         on cream / white     4.58 / 4.83
  ink-faint #80698A on cream             4.63
  plum              on sage-light        5.31
  white             on #9A4A34           6.17

FAILS - these four combinations are banned outright:

  Combination                                  Ratio      Use instead
  ------------------------------------------  ---------  ------------------------
  sage on cream / white                       2.87/3.02  sage-dark for text.
                                                          sage is fills, rings,
                                                          dots, chart strokes
  cream or white on sage / sage-light         2.87/3.02  plum on sage-light
                                              / 2.18     (5.31), or a sage/12
                                                          tint with sage-dark
  warm-gray on cream / white                  3.28/3.45  ink-muted for
                                                          secondary text.
                                                          warm-gray is icons,
                                                          disabled only
  danger as body text                         4.16       danger-text for the
                                                          text, danger for the
                                                          border and icon

Also banned: warm-gray-light on cream (2.06) and warm-gray-lighter on cream
(1.24). warm-gray-lighter is a BORDER COLOUR ONLY - never text, never an icon
that carries meaning.

BORDERLINE - allowed only at large text or UI sizes:
  terracotta on cream        4.00
  white on terracotta        4.21
  sage-dark on a sage/12 tint 3.96
If a tinted chip needs real text, use plum on the tint (9.26) or darken the tint
to sage/8.

THE GRADIENT PROBLEM
The original signature gradient ran plum -> terracotta. White text on that is
11.58:1 at the plum end but 4.21:1 at the terracotta end, so it fails. The seed
gradient ends at #9A4A34 instead, giving 11.58 -> 6.17 across the full sweep.
Use --brand-gradient. Do not re-add bare terracotta as a gradient endpoint for
anything carrying text. If you want a warm variant for large display type only,
plum -> terracotta at 24px and above is acceptable.

LOCKED TEXT COLOUR ASSIGNMENTS
  Body                                -> text-ink
  Secondary, descriptions, helper     -> text-ink-muted
  Meta, timestamps, axis labels       -> text-ink-faint
  Warm emphasis, specialty, streak    -> text-terracotta-dark
  Calm, success, grounded             -> text-sage-dark
  Errors                              -> text-danger-text inside a
                                         border-danger/30 bg-danger/5 box
  Text on plum or plum-light          -> text-cream
  Text on any sage fill               -> text-plum
  Labels inside tinted chips          -> text-plum unless the chip is
                                         plum-tinted

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6  MOTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

framer-motion, easing cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

  Enter     opacity 0 to 1, y 12 to 0, 350ms. Stagger children 40ms, max 5.
  Hover     y -2 on cards, scale 1.03 on buttons.
  Tap       scale 0.97.
  Reduced   prefers-reduced-motion respected everywhere, duration 0.
  No looping ambient animation. No pulsing hearts, no bobbing illustrations.
  A calm app does not move when you are not touching it.
  No layout-animating page transitions that push content. Cross-fade only.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 - ANTI-SLOP CONSTITUTION (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

This is the part that decides whether the result looks designed or looks
generated. Follow every rule.

FORBIDDEN COLOUR
  - No indigo, violet or purple-blue gradients. No #6366F1, #8B5CF6, #A855F7.
  - No blue as a UI colour. No sky-500, no cyan, no neon.
  - No emerald or green "success" chips (#22C55E). Use sage.
  - No dark-mode toggle. If any surface goes dark it is plum or plum-dark,
    never #0F172A slate.
  - No 18th colour. Every colour in the build is one of the 17 tokens or an
    alpha variant of one. No hardcoded hex in any component file.

FORBIDDEN CONTRAST PAIRINGS
  - text-sage on cream or white. 2.87:1. Use text-sage-dark.
  - Cream or white text on a sage or sage-light fill. 2.87 / 2.18:1. Text on
    sage is text-plum.
  - text-warm-gray for any text. 3.28:1. Use text-ink-muted.
  - text-danger for error copy. 4.16:1. Use text-danger-text.
  - Bare terracotta as a gradient endpoint behind white text. 4.21:1.
  - warm-gray-lighter as text or as a meaningful icon. 1.24:1.

FORBIDDEN TYPE
  - No Inter, no Geist, no system-ui default.
  - No font-black headings. No uppercase paragraphs. No more than one
    uppercase-eyebrow element per card.
  - No centred text blocks longer than two lines.
  - No 56px or larger hero type inside the app. The marketing landing page may
    go to 64px once.

FORBIDDEN LAYOUT CLICHES
  - No hero made of a big centred headline, subheadline, two buttons and an
    abstract blob graphic. The landing hero is left-aligned, split, with a real
    product preview.
  - No row of 3 or 4 identical icon+title+text feature cards. Features get
    different layouts: a wide row, a list, a split, a quote.
  - No row of 4 identical KPI cards with big numbers and green up-arrows. Stats
    are one card with an internal layout, or hairline-separated strips.
  - No icon-in-a-rounded-square tile above every heading. One accent element per
    section, and sometimes none.
  - No equal-height 3-across card grid as the default page shape. Use
    lg:grid-cols-[1.15fr_.85fr] asymmetry or vertical lists.
  - No sidebar + 4 metric cards + table + chart dashboard template.
  - No max-w-4xl mx-auto text-center for content pages.
  - No floating action button unless the page is genuinely a compose surface.

FORBIDDEN DECORATION
  - No 3D or isometric illustrations, no stock-photo grids, no Unsplash
    hotlinks. If an image is truly needed use a CSS or SVG abstract shape in
    our palette.
  - No glassmorphism on every surface. No neon glow. No gradient borders.
  - No animated gradient text except the wordmark.
  - No confetti. No sparkle icons scattered as decoration.
  - No fake SaaS data. No "Trusted by 10,000+ students", no "4.9/5 from 2,000
    reviews", no fabricated logo wall, no pricing table. Testimonials, if any,
    are plain text quotes with an initial avatar, and only on the landing page.

FORBIDDEN COMPONENTS AND CODE SMELLS
  - No default shadcn Card or Dialog look. Restyle or rebuild, do not leave
    defaults visible.
  - No alert(), no confirm(), no TODO in shipped UI.
  - No horizontal scroll on desktop.
  - No layout shift on load. Reserve space for async content with skeletons at
    the exact final size.
  - No danger red for "cancel". Cancel is ghost.

COPY
  - Plain, warm, second person. Short sentences. No marketing throat-clearing.
  - Banned phrases: "elevate", "unlock", "journey", "holistic", "empower",
    "game-changer", "you are not alone in this journey", "dive in", "take care
    of yourself", "start your free trial".
  - Banned emoji in body copy.
  - Numbers are specific and believable: "6 check-ins this week", not "84% of
    your potential".
  - Error copy says what happened and what to do next. Never "Something went
    wrong."

SECTION 4 - RESPONSIVE CONTRACT
================================================================================

MOBILE  (under 768)

  bar above the nav.

TABLET  (768 to 1023)

  grids may become one column.

DESKTOP  (1024 and above)
  240px SideNav plus TopBar. No BottomNav, no MobileHeader. Content max-width
  1080px, 24px padding. Asymmetric grids (1.15fr / 0.85fr) are welcome. Optional
  288px right rail for contextual panels: chat detail, counsellor profile,
  journal metadata.

Tap targets 44px and above everywhere. pb-safe on anything sitting above the
bottom nav. No horizontal scroll at 320px width.
